## The image

A giant, graceful manta ray bursts out of a huge open storybook resting on
a wooden desk. The book's pages curl upward and turn into real ocean waves,
while droplets and tiny golden stars fly through warm lamplight. A small
child reader leans back in awe. Charming storybook watercolor, rich deep
blues against warm gold, a dynamic diagonal composition that reads instantly
even at small size. No text, no watermark, no photorealism.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 stdlib only, deterministic — no network, no clock, no
randomness) copies the finished artwork `image.jpg` (1024×1024) byte-for-byte
to `/output/entry.png`.
